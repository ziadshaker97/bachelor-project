# Probation and Benefits Policy

The standard probation period for new employees is three months from the start date unless a different period is stated in the employment contract or local HR guidance.

During probation, employees are expected to complete mandatory onboarding modules, attend manager check-ins, and meet the role expectations defined for their department.

Benefits enrollment, medical coverage start dates, and payroll-related questions should be confirmed with HR operations because plan timing may vary by contract type and location.
