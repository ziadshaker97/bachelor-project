# Work Schedule Policy

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM from Sunday through Thursday for full-time employees unless the employment agreement or department schedule states otherwise.

Employees are expected to be available during the team's core collaboration window of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM so meetings, manager check-ins, and onboarding sessions can be scheduled consistently.

If a team uses a shift, hybrid, or client-aligned schedule, the employee's manager confirms the expected hours during onboarding and documents any exceptions with HR operations.
