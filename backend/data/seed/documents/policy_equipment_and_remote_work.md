# Equipment and Remote Work Policy

Employees who need a laptop, monitor, headset, or other onboarding equipment should submit a workplace equipment request through the help desk portal after manager confirmation.

Remote or hybrid work expectations are defined by the employee's department and manager. New hires should confirm their on-site days, device pickup instructions, and collaboration expectations during the first-week check-in.

Urgent equipment issues that block onboarding work should be escalated through IT support and copied to the employee's manager.
